Like every web server, they receive your IP address, the address of the page, your browser's user agent and the time, in order to deliver the page and to protect the service against attacks. We have not enabled any analytics or logging of visits. Cloudflare's own handling of this data is described in its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## No cookies, no analytics, no third parties

- The website sets no cookies.
- It runs no analytics, tracking or advertising code.
- Fonts and libraries are served from this website itself, so your browser contacts no other server.
- Links to GitHub and other websites take you to those websites, where their own privacy policies apply.

## What stays in your browser

To remember your reading progress, bookmarks, notes, quiz results and preferences, the website stores them in your browser's local storage, under the key `aeb-state-v1`. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can export it, import it or delete it under **Preferences** on the website, or delete it with your browser's "clear site data" function.

The "Improve with your agent" button copies a text to your clipboard on your own device. Nothing is sent.

## Contributing

Contributions happen on GitHub, where GitHub's privacy statement applies. When you contribute, your GitHub name appears in the public history of the project and in the credits of the pages you shaped.
