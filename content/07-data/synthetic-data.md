> [!tldr]
> Synthetic data is generated by models to train or evaluate other models: instructions, conversations, reasoning traces, code with tests, rewritten documents. It works when it is diverse, verified and filtered, and mixed with real data. Unfiltered or recursive generation narrows distributions and amplifies errors.

## Why it matters
Synthetic data filled the gap left by scarce human data: much of modern post-training data is generated, and pretraining increasingly includes rewritten or synthetic text. For product teams, it is a fast way to bootstrap evals and fine-tuning sets.

## Intuition
Synthetic data is a practice exam written by a teacher. A good teacher writes varied, correct questions; a lazy one rephrases the same question, and students learn only that.

## How it works
**Generation methods.** Self-Instruct (Wang et al., 2022) bootstraps instructions from seeds; Evol-Instruct makes them harder; persona-driven generation diversifies; rejection sampling keeps only verified solutions; distillation uses stronger models’ outputs. See [distillation](#distillation-merging).

**Verification.** For math and code, check answers and run tests; for other tasks, use calibrated judges and human spot checks. See [LLM-as-judge](#llm-as-judge).

**Model collapse.** Training repeatedly on a model’s own outputs can narrow and degrade the distribution (Shumailov et al., 2024); mixing in real data and filtering for diversity counteract it.

**Phi-style curricula.** Microsoft’s Phi models showed that carefully generated “textbook” data can train strong small models.

## In practice
- Seed generation with real examples and diverse personas or topics.
- Verify, deduplicate and filter; keep a small fraction.
- Mix with real data, and evaluate on real held-out data only.
- Check provider terms before training on generated outputs.

## Numbers to know
> [!number]
> Rules of thumb. Expect to keep a minority of generated examples after rigorous filtering. Always evaluate on real data the generator never saw.

## Pitfalls and anti-patterns
> [!pitfall] Evaluating on synthetic data from the same generator
> The model is graded on questions written in its own style.

## Talk like an expert
The vocabulary: **self-instruct**, **evol-instruct**, **rejection sampling**, **persona-driven generation**, **verification**, **model collapse**, **data diversity**. Experts report how synthetic data was verified and what share was kept. The live debate is how far synthetic data can replace human data as high-quality text runs short.

> [!hottake] Verified synthetic data will be the main fuel of model improvement this decade.
> **The counterargument:** synthetic data mostly redistributes what models already know; genuinely new knowledge and human preferences still require real-world data.
